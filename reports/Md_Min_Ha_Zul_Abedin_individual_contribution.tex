I contributed equally to the project. My primary responsibility was the
representation-learning pipeline and the first-pass manuscript draft.

\section*{Code and experiments}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Audited and cleaned the 28-source pre-training pool: removed duplicate
        archives, excluded evaluation datasets, filtered unsuitable resolutions,
        converted grayscale images to RGB, and created WebDataset shards.
  \item Implemented bounded multi-scale cropping and the DINO-style
        student--teacher objective with EMA updates, initialized from DINOv3
        ViT-Base/16.
  \item Implemented LP-FT, LLRD ($\gamma=0.75$), $256\times256$ inputs, and
        horizontal-flip TTA; organized the training configurations and scripts.
  \item Ran the main MedicinalPlant transfer experiments, tracked training
        curves, and prepared the pipeline and training-dynamics figures.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Writing and analysis}
I drafted the abstract, Introduction, Methodology, and portions of the
Discussion. I wrote the corpus-audit and training descriptions, prepared figure
captions, checked notation and reported values, and helped interpret the
cumulative ablation without presenting it as independent causal evidence.

\section*{Shared work}
With Md Mehedi Hasan, I selected the research questions, reviewed the
literature, checked the final numbers, edited the complete paper, and verified
the final LaTeX/PDF output.

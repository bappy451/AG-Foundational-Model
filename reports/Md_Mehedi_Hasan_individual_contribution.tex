I contributed equally to the project. My primary responsibility was downstream
evaluation, benchmark validation, and the second-pass manuscript draft.

\section*{Code and experiments}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Prepared the MedicinalPlant and PlantSeg layouts, verified the
        stratified train/validation split, and organized the deterministic
        evaluation harness.
  \item Implemented and ran the random-initialization and ImageNet-21k baseline
        configurations, documenting the difference between direct fine-tuning
        and Agri-DINO's LP-FT protocol.
  \item Implemented the cumulative ablations for resolution, LP warmup, LLRD,
        and horizontal-flip TTA.
  \item Audited the PlantSeg YOLO-to-classification conversion, traced the
        sample-level filename parsing, and identified the inflation from 20
        disease categories to 300 micro-classes.
  \item Debugged evaluation scripts and checked class counts, labels,
        preprocessing, input resolution, and metric computation.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Analysis and tables}
I ran the baseline, ablation, and PlantSeg audit experiments. I analyzed
validation accuracy, validation loss, training--validation gaps, and the
LP-to-LLRD transition; prepared the main-results, ablation, training-dynamics,
and PlantSeg tables; and checked the arithmetic and consistency of the reported
values, captions, and claims.

\section*{Writing and shared work}
I drafted Related Work, Experiments, the PlantSeg analysis, and portions of the
Discussion and Conclusion. I also edited the full manuscript for readability,
protocol clarity, and reproducibility. With Md Min-Ha-Zul Abedin, I reviewed the
literature, verified the final numbers, debugged the pipeline, and checked the
final LaTeX/PDF output.
